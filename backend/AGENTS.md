# Backend notes

- Caja diaria uses the `America/Managua` business date. Keep its UTC boundary conversion and advisory lock shared by sale creation and closure so a sale cannot race a close.
- Monthly brand settlements use a fixed 20% store commission on product subtotals. The terminal's 5% POS charge is reported separately and does not reduce the brand payout.
- Only completed months can be settled. A settlement is unique per brand and month; a payment must retain its date and transfer reference.
- Brand rent uses a per-brand monthly configuration and explicit per-month generation. Each generated charge is unique per brand and month, charges the full monthly amount in the start month, snapshots the configured amount, and records partial payments with date and reference.
- The monthly report includes sales by day, brand, product, and payment method, plus comparison with the previous month. POS fees are allocated back to brands and products proportionally in cents.
- Settlement exports are selected by brand and month as a single XLSX statement with a summary tab and a detailed sales tab. The detailed tab has one row per sold product and includes sale date, customer, payment methods and amounts, SKU, units, unit price, discount, line subtotal, and sale total. Its totals row sums units, discounts, and line subtotals; sale totals count each transaction once. Keep the summary's per-product/payment-method detail, totals, POS fee, store commission, net payout, and settlement status; use white headers, gold accents, and brand initials if its optional logo is unavailable. Inventory templates use the same white and gold palette.
- Sales history is exposed at `GET /api/v1/pos/ventas/historial`; keep its date filters in the Managua business timezone, and preserve brand, payment-method, customer/product search, pagination, and sale-item detail.
- Client records use `nombre_cliente`, `telefono`, `correo`, and optional `fecha_nacimiento`. POS sales must use the selected client ID; when none is selected, use the shared `Consumidor Final` customer because `ventas.cliente_id` is required.
- POS sale lines accept an optional edited unit price and either a fixed per-unit discount or a percentage discount. Validate both server-side, store the total currency discount in `venta_detalles.descuento`, and use the discounted line subtotal for payment and settlement totals.
- Receipt imports can regenerate a printable QR PDF at `GET /api/v1/recepcion/{ingreso_id}/qr-pdf`; generate from products attached to that import and preserve their existing QR values.
- Individual QR PNGs and printable PDF labels share a white rounded-card layout with centered QR, product name, SKU, and bold C$ price; preserve the existing QR payload and use scalable font fallbacks when system fonts are missing.
- Inventory search accepts SKU, name, QR code, and description; keep the global header and inventory page searches wired to that endpoint.
- Generic inventory template downloads include one Excel per configured rubro in a ZIP. Each is reusable for any brand with the same rubro; preserve the rubro header checks when importing.
- Generic and brand inventory templates use the compact field layout configured per rubro; imports map those columns into product name, size, and rubro details while retaining compatibility with the prior standard template layout.
- Brand inventory template imports return the imported products' QR labels as the same editable `.docx` sheet used by catalog reprints; receipt QR PDF regeneration remains available separately.
